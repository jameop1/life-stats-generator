# LIFE STATS — Pixel Age & Metric Calculator

Enter your birth date to calculate fascinating, cosmic, and biological life statistics rendered in nostalgic pixel-art aesthetics.

## Features

- **Biological & Daily Metrics**: See cumulative estimates of heartbeats, breaths, blinks, hours slept, and steps taken.
- **Planetary & Temporal Perspective**: Discover your age in planetary orbits (Mars, Venus, Jupiter), moon phases witnessed, and total solar rotations.
- **Pixel Art UI**: Vibrant 8-bit retro gaming typography, animations, and sound effects.
- **Shareable Snapshots**: Export summary visual cards to share on social media.
- **Local & Offline**: Runs entirely within your browser without storing personal details.

## Getting Started

Open `index.html` in any modern web browser or try it on https://jameop1.github.io/life-stats-generator/
